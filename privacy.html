Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

A.A. Mudanya & Co. Advocates ("we", "our", "the firm") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact details you submit through our forms, such as name, email address, phone number and the content of your message.
  • Appointment request details, including preferred date, time and practice area of interest.
  • Messages you send through our website chatbot or WhatsApp link.
  • Basic technical information such as browser type and general usage patterns, used to maintain and improve the site.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries and schedule consultations.
  • To provide legal services once a client relationship is established.
  • To send legal updates or firm news, where you have subscribed to our newsletter.
  • To improve our website and client communication.

3. Confidentiality

Any information shared with us in connection with a legal matter is treated in accordance with advocate-client privilege and professional confidentiality obligations under Kenyan law.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share information with third-party service providers who assist us in operating our website, or where required by law.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information you share with us against unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information held by us by contacting us using the details below.
